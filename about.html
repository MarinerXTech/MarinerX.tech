Built from practical boat problems

MarinerX builds practical sailing tools that solve specific problems without adding unnecessary complexity.

Our Mission

Develop useful, well-engineered products that make small-boat ownership simpler, cleaner, and more reliable.

Our Story

MarinerX started as a hands-on engineering project. The first product, the MarinerX Hull Dryer, came from the problem of trapped hull moisture and the need for a cleaner drying tool.

Our Philosophy

Understand the problem clearly, prototype thoughtfully, test in the real world, and refine until the solution works the way it should.
