Spar Mount Beta Test

Help test a cleaner way to carry Sunfish spars on deck.

I've been testing a padded spar transport kit for trailering a Sunfish with the spars carried on deck. The front mount drops into the mast step for repeatable placement, while the rear mount supports the spar bundle. Foam padding protects the deck and spars, and the kit includes integrated straps and hooks.

Initial road testing has gone well, and I'm looking for a small group of careful beta testers before deciding whether to offer it more broadly.

Who this is for

This beta is best for sailors who trailer a Sunfish and want a more repeatable way to carry spars on the deck. You should be comfortable inspecting your own trailering setup and giving practical feedback.

Beta note: This kit is intended to support, locate, and secure the spar bundle as part of a responsible trailering setup. Users are responsible for confirming that their boat, spars, and gear are safely secured before driving.
