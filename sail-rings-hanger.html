Sail Rings FAQ

Will these sail rings fit a Sunfish?

Yes. They are designed for the 1.5-inch spars used on Sunfish rigs and combine sail attachment with mainsheet support at the same rigging point.

What spar diameter do they fit?

The rings fit 1.5-inch-diameter spars. That size is also used on Sailfish, Minifish, Phantom and Scorpion rigs, along with similar small sailboats. Measure your spar before ordering if the rig has been modified or replaced.

Why is the mainsheet hanger integrated into the sail ring?

Combining the two functions creates a mainsheet-support point without adding a separate hanger at the same location. The channel helps keep the sheet elevated, organized and less likely to catch during maneuvers while the ring continues to secure the sail.
