Rig Nest

Secure your spars. Protect your deck. Simplify transport.

Rig transport and storage

A secure place for your rig to ride.

Rig Nest anchors into your mast step to create a clean tie-down point for a Sunfish or similar small dinghy rig. It is built for trailering, storage, and reducing the on-shore scramble around spars and sail bundles.

Mast-step mount No drilling Glass-filled ASA Modular accessories

Key Benefits

  • Mounts directly into your mast step with no drilling required
  • Keeps your rig stable and upright for trailering or storage
  • Made from marine-grade glass fiber-reinforced ASA
  • Designed for modular accessories
  • Built for Sunfish and other small sailboats with a 2" diameter mast

Beta Status

Rig Nest is still in beta. The current focus is real-world fit, trailering behavior, and accessory feedback before it becomes a normal cart product.
