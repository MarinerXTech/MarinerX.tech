Hull Dryer Questions

How do I know if it will fit my boat?

The Hull Dryer family supports several common inspection-port sizes and brands. The Standard is intended for 6-inch ports and the Mini for smaller 4- and 5-inch applications, with direct-fit and adapter options depending on the port brand and style. Use the Boat Inspection Port & Deck Plate Identification Guide to match your port before ordering.

How do I dry water trapped inside a boat hull?

First remove any pooled water that can be drained or pumped out. For the moisture that remains in enclosed hull spaces, continuous air exchange helps evaporation by replacing humid air with drier outside air. The MarinerX Hull Dryer boat-drying system uses an inspection-port opening to move air into the hull; a second open port or vent gives that air a path out.

Will the Hull Dryer work on a Sunfish?

Yes. Sunfish hull drying is a primary use case, and air can circulate around the internal foam blocks. Because inspection ports can be added or replaced over a boat's life, confirm the port size, brand and engagement style with the Sunfish inspection-port fit guide before choosing a Hull Dryer or adapter.

What is the difference between the Hull Dryer and Hull Dryer Mini?

The Standard is intended for 6-inch ports and moves 101 CFM; it measures 6.5 × 6.5 × 7.25 inches. The Mini is intended for 4- and 5-inch ports and moves 39 CFM; it measures 4.5 × 4.5 × 7 inches. Both use 12V DC power and an IP68-rated fan. Compare Hull Dryer Standard and Mini ordering options.

What inspection ports does the Hull Dryer fit?

The Standard directly fits 6-inch Beckson and West Marine screw-in ports, while the Mini directly fits their 4-inch screw-in ports. Adapters support selected RWO, Allen, Sea-Dog and Seaflo ports, plus some additional sizes. Quarter-turn and pop-out ports are not universally compatible, so use the complete inspection-port compatibility guide for the current fit matrix.

Can I power the Hull Dryer from USB-C?

Yes, with the available 12V USB-C Power Delivery adapter cable and a USB-C PD power supply or battery bank that supports 12V output. A basic USB source that cannot negotiate 12V will not work. The cable converts USB-C PD to the Hull Dryer's barrel-plug connection; see the Hull Dryer USB-C power adapter in the power accessories section.

What are the power requirements?

The Hull Dryer runs on 12VDC and draws up to 1.2 amps at full speed. It comes with a standard wall plug (110VAC to 12VDC), but you can also power it directly using:

  • Any regulated 12VDC source
  • Barrel jack: 5.5mm x 2.1mm, center-positive
  • Protection features: reverse polarity protection and a built-in PTC fuse
  • Do not supply more than 12VDC

This makes it compatible with many battery packs, solar setups, or marine power systems - just ensure the voltage and polarity are correct.

Can I run the Hull Dryer from solar?

Yes, if the solar setup provides regulated 12V DC output rated for at least 1.2A. Do not connect directly to an unregulated solar panel.

How weather-resistant is it?

The Hull Dryer is built for harsh marine environments. Key protections include:

  • IP68-rated fan - dustproof and waterproof
  • Sealed wiring
  • Outer casing and louvers designed to shed rain and spray

For best results, avoid leaving it exposed in horizontal setups or fully submerged conditions. It's weather-resistant, not submersible.

Will it work on boats that are foam-filled?

It depends on whether air can circulate through the space. Boats like the Sunfish have foam blocks but still allow airflow, so they dry effectively. Fully foam-filled hulls with no airflow may not benefit - but other compartments on the boat might.

Can it run horizontally?

Yes, it threads in securely and runs fine. Just note the louvers shed water best when upright, so in horizontal setups, avoid rain exposure.

Does it blow air in or pull it out?

It blows air into the hull. A second outlet port allows air to escape, preventing pressure buildup or vacuum. The fan moves air gently at low static pressure for safe and effective drying.

Can it be used for winterizing or storage?

It's a great end-of-season tool to remove trapped moisture before long-term storage.
